The Echo of Ironwood

A tale of memory, loss, and rediscovery beneath the ancient trees.

In the heart of the forgotten Ironwood Forest, a reclusive botanist stumbles upon a journal buried beneath decades of leaves and decay. The Echo of Ironwood follows her journey to uncover the truth behind the journal’s author — a child who vanished in 1936 — and the haunting parallels to her own past. With each page, she ventures deeper into a mystery rooted in grief, resilience, and the strange ways nature remembers.

Chapter One: The Hollow Path

Dr. Elin Rowe arrives at the edge of Ironwood with a bag full of field tools and a mind clouded by unanswered questions. The trees seem to whisper as she sets camp, their branches swaying without wind. An encounter with a fox bearing a worn ribbon around its neck leads her to a clearing unlike any other. Here, she finds the journal — its first page dated exactly eighty-nine years before her arrival.

Chapter Two: Rings of the Forgotten

Elin deciphers the child’s words, each entry darker than the last. The boy wrote of voices in the bark, of dreams that bled into waking life. As her nights grow restless and the forest tightens around her, Elin begins to question the boundaries of memory. Are the dreams her own, or has the forest chosen her as its next historian?
